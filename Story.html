The Three Sovereigns

In an age when steel sang and sorcery ran deep, three sovereigns held the realm.

  • The Steadfast — Aldric of Velmora
  • The Untamed — Kaelen of Duskreach
  • The Queen — Seraphine of Aurelia

The Blight

For generations the lands rested in quiet harmony under their watch.

Then a creeping blight seeped into the soil and the blood alike. Those it touched swelled beyond all natural measure.

Garden hounds turned ravenous, citizens forgot their own names, and field mice grew large enough to drag travelers down.

The Reckoning

The three drew their blades and marched against the corrupted horde.

Kaelen fell in the second week, his body consumed by the very rot he fought.

Yet from the ashes of that defeat, Aldric forged an order — sworn hunters bound to purge the blight from every corner of the land. Their expeditions uncovered old wards and forgotten cures.

Seraphine, meanwhile, opened the council halls to common folk.

Together their efforts gave the kingdoms something they had not felt in years: a reason to hold the line. Still the blight spreads... few remain willing to walk into its shadow.
